The Youkai Gift
=== 4937
The Youkai Mountain Memory is a tech demo for GIC's future enemy complexity. Two abilities are not seen anywhere else:
^orange;Counter Stances^reset; - Enemies/Bosses are able to enter a stance which if they are attacked, will parry the attack whilst counter-attacking in the process. While existing enemies can block, none are capable of countering.
^orange;Unblockables^reset; - Bosses can perform attacks that must be avoided altogether. Footwork and jumping must be exercised here with some attacks.
