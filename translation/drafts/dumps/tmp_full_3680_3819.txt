=== 3804
Most weapons have a durability system that affects 2 stats: Weapon Proficiency and Jam Chance. The lower the durability of a firearm, the worse its general performance will be. Lower weapon proficiency will often mean the gun will miss more as well as gain higher spread deviation. It will also mean a higher chance for the gun to jam. Weapons that have crits will instead have lower crit-rates as its durability lowers. Weapons with durability can be repaired by holding a repair kit and right-clicking on the weapon.
