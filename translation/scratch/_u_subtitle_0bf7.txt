


 Thanks to subsidies from colonial governments and certain administrators, engineers like this one 
 only require that clients bring specific parts and the host item for conversion or modification. 
 Engineered Munitions can provide effective situational benefits against a variety of targets.
 
 These services give the average adventurer an edge in particularly dangerous territories, 
 -perhaps so that they might bring something of value back to the colony. 
 
 Faction Embassies sometimes sell pre-engineered items that cannot be crafted here.
