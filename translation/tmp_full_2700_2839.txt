=== 2819
GIC's heavy reliance on permanent world states comes with the cost of new characters not being able to replay through most quests if the world state isn't reset beforehand. In order to reset, you will need to go to your /starbound/storage/universe/- folder and delete the file called 'universe.dat'. This will reset world states but also delete any bookmarks. It's this reason that it's intended to be done at the same time as starting a new character/run.
